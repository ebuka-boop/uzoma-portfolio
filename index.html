ABOUT

I’ve worked across dozens of ecommerce brands, partnering with strategy teams on campaigns, flows, segmentation, signup experiences and the systems behind them.

I’m equally comfortable inside Figma, Klaviyo and a design critique — and I care as much about making a process repeatable as I do about making the final creative memorable.
